# Code by Emazouz — Full-Stack Web Developer

**Websites that turn visitors into customers.** 🚀

I'm a full-stack web developer based in Morocco. I build fast, mobile-first websites and online stores for businesses, startups and store owners — designed to sell, not just to look good.

A site that gets traffic but no sales usually fails three tests: **speed**, **clarity** (a visitor should understand your offer in 5 seconds) and **a clear next step**. Fixing those three is exactly what I do.

## 🛠️ What I build

- **Business & company websites** — fast, credible, built to generate enquiries
- **E-commerce stores** — product catalogues, cart, online payment (Stripe), order management
- **Landing pages** — focused pages for one offer and one action
- **Admin dashboards & CMS** — manage your content, products and orders yourself
- **Authentication & accounts** — sign-up, login, email verification, password reset, OAuth
